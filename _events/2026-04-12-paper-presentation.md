Here we are with another poorly timed event which we have to conduct anyway: Paper presentation.


You have to form a team (preferably 3-4 members), select 3 papers (and the order of priority) from the drive [link](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1dmzRgG8zvqBKqAybx9SRTgb_1UiYC88m)

In the drive folder, there is a README file containing a brief introduction for each paper, please go through it.  For some papers, there are specific instructions (also duly mentioned)

If you want to present a paper which is NOT listed in the drive, send it to one of the convenors via mail BEFORE 25th March who will then accept/reject your proposal.

We will shortly release a form to fill in the finalised list of papers and team members. 

You will have to prepare a 15 minute presentation (strictly adhere to the timeline.) And each presentation will be followed up by a short QnA session.

Deadline for decided papers and team is 28th March

Presentations will be held on 12th April.

Date: **12th April, 2026 (Sunday)**  
Time: **10:30 AM to 12 PM, 2 to 4 PM**  
Venue: **G21, OPB**
